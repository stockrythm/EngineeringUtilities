Importing into TiddlyWiki

  1. Download the import file.
  2. Drag it onto your open wiki (or use the Import button in the Tools tab).
  3. Review the list, then click Import.

Reusing sections with Shiraz

With splitting on, the parent tiddler calls <<details>> once per section and transcludes the section tiddler as its src. Point other pages at the same section tiddler to reuse it. If your Shiraz version expects different parameters, switch to a custom template.

Check the report

Anything that TiddlyWiki can't express exactly (code inside list items, raw HTML, oversized tables) is listed in the Report tab with the line it came from.
